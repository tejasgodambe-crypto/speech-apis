Scope and evidence rules

This is a broad, source-constrained research inventory checked on 21 September 2026, not a guarantee that every commercial reseller or newly launched API has been found. Each confirmed row has official evidence of at least one Indian language or explicit Indian English. Shunya zero-med is clearly marked conditional because its official “Indic” claim is not enumerated. Model variants share a row only where useful; separate domain products have separate rows.

NV means not verified in the material retrieved in this review, not “unsupported”, “unavailable”, “free”, or “no customers”. An NV cell’s link identifies material reviewed and does not prove absence. No private sales terms, unlisted prices or customer deployments were inferred. Source links are included in the relevant cells. Vendor benchmarks are not independently validated.

Indian languages includes scheduled languages and explicitly documented regional languages/dialects; Nepali and Urdu count regardless of whether a vendor uses a Nepal/Pakistan locale. Generic “English” or “all accents” is not silently relabeled Indian English. A language supported by one model/mode is not automatically assigned to another.

Access vocabulary: REST sync = one request/final response; REST async batch = submit job then poll/callback; WebSocket live = bidirectional live audio; gRPC live = streaming RPC; local SDK = in-process/offline API. Streaming an output from an uploaded file is different from live-input ASR. SDK wrappers are not additional deployment types.

Deployment vocabulary: managed cloud; private cloud/VPC; on-premises; edge/offline; hybrid. Distribution is direct vendor API, government platform, cloud marketplace, container registry or downloadable SDK/model. A marketplace listing is not evidence that inference runs on the buyer’s premises.

Costs: quoted in the vendor’s published currency and unit, without FX conversion. Per-hour conversions are explicitly labeled calculated. Prices exclude unspecified tax, ancillary storage/egress/compute, add-ons and negotiated terms. A quote or NV is retained where a model/region/mode-specific numeric amount could not be established. Rates are not an all-in total-cost ranking.
